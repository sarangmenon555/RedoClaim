> Every year, lakhs of policyholders get rejection letters they don't understand, deadlines they don't know exist, and rights they never exercise. Most just give up. The insurer counts on it.
> RedoClaim exists so you don't.

---

## What is RedoClaim?

RedoClaim is an AI-powered insurance claim analysis tool built specifically for Indian policyholders. Upload your rejection letter, your policy PDF, or your Customer Information Sheet — and get a full IRDAI Master Circular 2024-compliant audit, a mapped violation report, and AI-drafted appeal letters ready to send — in minutes.

---

## The Problem

Indian health insurance claim rejections are rampant — and most of them are either wrongful, procedurally defective, or in direct violation of IRDAI regulations. Yet:

- Policyholders rarely know their rights under the **IRDAI Master Circular on Health Insurance, 2024**
- GRO and Ombudsman deadlines (15 days, 30 days) pass silently
- Appeal letters without regulatory citations are almost always ignored
- Insurers rely on policyholder inaction as a business strategy

RedoClaim changes the power balance.

## Features

### Policy Analyzer
Extracts and flags risky clauses from your policy PDF — waiting periods, sub-limits, room rent caps, co-payment triggers, and hidden exclusions — before they're used against you.

### CIS Scanner
Scans your Customer Information Sheet (CIS) and maps every inclusion and exclusion to **IRDAI Master Circular 2024, Para 4.2** — so you know exactly what you're entitled to.

### Rejection Auditor
Runs a structured **3-step IRDAI Hierarchy of Evidence audit**:
1. **SLA Violations** — Did the insurer breach mandated turnaround times?
2. **Regulatory Violations** — Does the rejection violate IRDAI Master Circular provisions?
3. **Redressal Route** — GRO, Insurance Ombudsman, IRDAI Bima Bharosa, or Consumer Court?

### Appeal Drafter
Generates ready-to-send legal drafts:
- GRO (Grievance Redressal Officer) letters
- Insurance Ombudsman complaints
- E-Daakhil portal drafts
- All citations pulled directly from IRDAI regulations

### timeline Tracker
Never miss a deadline again. Tracks and alerts you on:
- 15-day GRO response window
- 30-day insurer TAT
- Ombudsman filing deadlines
- Consumer Court limitation periods

### Portability Advisor
Know your portability rights under **IRDAI Health Insurance Regulation 17** — waiting period credits, moratorium transfers, and a step-by-step switching guide.
